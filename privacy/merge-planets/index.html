01. Developer & contact information

Bloede Feainne Games develops Merge Planets: Make a Galaxy!, an Android physics puzzle game made with Unity, package com.feainne.mergeplanets. This policy describes the Google Play Games-enabled release. Website visits and support email are explained separately.

For privacy enquiries or game support, email bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com.

02. What information is processed

The game stores progress locally and uses Google Play Games Services for sign-in, leaderboards, achievements and cloud saves. These online features transmit information to Google. Local gameplay remains available if sign-in or a connection is unavailable.

  • Player identity: the Google Play Games Player ID and gamer display name. The Google plugin also receives the profile's avatar URL; Google's own interface may display the gamer profile. The game does not ask for your Google password or request your email address, contacts or friends list.
  • Gameplay: completed-run scores for the Highest Score leaderboard and the First Fusion achievement. Pending submissions can be stored locally and retried later.
  • Cloud saves: best score, earned achievement state and a versioned game snapshot containing score, planet levels, positions and movement, current and next planet, gameplay timers, and save-revision metadata. These are linked to the signed-in Play Games player. Sound, vibration and language preferences are not included in our cloud-save payload.
  • Google SDK information: Google documents automatic analytics and diagnostics for SDK stability and improvement, and gamer identity processing. Its documentation does not enumerate every technical field for this game's pinned native SDK version. We do not represent Google's SDK as telemetry-free or claim a particular hardware or advertising identifier is collected without evidence. See Google's Play Games data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

The game has no integrated advertising, in-app purchases, Firebase, or separate studio analytics service. Unity Analytics and Unity diagnostic reporting remain disabled; this does not disable Google's SDK processing.

03. Local gameplay storage

Unity's PlayerPrefs stores your best score, sound and vibration choices, and selected game language. Without a saved language choice, the game reads the device's interface language to choose a supported language.

Local files preserve the unfinished board and gameplay timers, with recovery copies for interrupted writes. Separate synchronization records keep the linked Player ID, queued scores, earned achievement, upload acknowledgements and save revisions. The display name and avatar URL are not written into our save-file format. An earlier device snapshot may be retained when a cloud game replaces it.

Local play and saving continue when online services fail. An unexpected shutdown may recover an earlier checkpoint. A restart or game over clears the active run, but does not erase best score, achievement, synchronization records, recovery copies or Google-held data.

Android backup: Android may back up or transfer app data depending on device and account settings. These copies are separate from Play Games Saved Games. The game does not operate a studio backup server or control Android backup retention. See Android's backup documentation.

04. Google, Unity & other services

Google Play Games: the game includes Google's Unity plugin 2.2.1 and native Play Games Services v2 SDK 22.0.0. Authentication is attempted automatically. After successful sign-in, score, achievement and cloud synchronization can occur automatically; the Sync button is not the only way transmission starts. There is currently no in-game switch that disables every Google SDK operation. The sign-in interface may offer to create a Google-managed Play Games profile; the studio does not create a separate username/password account. Google account and Play Games controls are managed through Google, and local play remains available without a successful sign-in.

Google processes service data under its applicable terms and privacy policies. Leaderboards and achievements may show your gamer identity and game results to others according to Google's features and your profile visibility settings. Cloud snapshots are associated with the signed-in player's game data, not offered as a public leaderboard record.

Unity: the engine and local packages provide rendering, input, localization and asset loading. Unity Engine Diagnostics, Unity Analytics, Cloud Diagnostics reporting and Performance Reporting are disabled, and no Unity Cloud project is linked. The Unity player privacy policy is provider information, not a statement that these disabled services are enabled.

Website: Privacy Policy opens this page in an external browser using a fixed URL without player information appended. GitHub Pages receives normal web requests, including IP addresses for security; see GitHub's hosting information. Our website has no analytics or tracking scripts.

Support email: if you email the studio, we receive your address, sender name if supplied, message, attachments and normal email metadata. Microsoft Outlook processes correspondence under its Privacy Statement. This exchange takes place outside the game.

The translation-feedback destination remains unconfigured. AdMob and a one-time Remove Ads purchase are planned for a later update and are not active integrations in this release.

05. Purposes of processing & sharing

Local processing runs gameplay, remembers preferences and restores progress. Google receives the information needed to authenticate the player, associate game data with that player, update rankings and achievements, and synchronize or restore saves. Google's documented SDK analytics and diagnostics support SDK reliability and product improvement.

The studio operates no new player-data backend. This does not mean data stays on the device: Google's services store online game data, and permitted leaderboard/profile information can be visible to other players. The game does not add an advertising or data-sale integration.

Support correspondence is used to respond to your enquiry, provide relevant help and follow-up, and handle privacy requests. Website hosting, Google services, email and operating-system backups are also subject to their respective providers' policies.

06. Data retention & deletion

On your device: active checkpoints are replaced as play progresses. Best score, preferences, earned achievement, queued submissions, synchronization metadata and recovery copies have no fixed automatic expiry. Clear this app's storage in Android settings to remove these local records together. Uninstalling normally removes app storage, but does not remove Google-held data, support email or all possible backup copies.

Google-held game data: use the Google Play Games profile controls to delete individual game data or your Play Games profile. Google's deletion instructions explain the available web and Play Store routes. For individual game data, choose Merge Planets: Make a Galaxy! in the game-data deletion list. Google controls its processing times, retention exceptions and any separately managed Play History; we do not promise a fixed deletion period or the deletion of all Google service records.

Preventing old progress from returning: stop the game on all devices and review Google's game sign-in/access settings before deleting online data. Clear the game's local storage on those devices and review Android backups as well. Deleting only the cloud copy leaves local saves and queued uploads; later sign-in or synchronization can recreate game data from those copies. Restarting a run, going offline, or removing access alone does not delete previously transmitted information.

The current game has no dedicated delete-cloud-data button and the studio does not operate a separate player-account database. Emailing the studio does not let us erase your Google account or remotely clear your device. We can help identify the relevant controls.

Studio correspondence: email bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com to request access, correction or deletion of messages held by the studio. Correspondence is retained as needed for the enquiry, related follow-up or applicable legal obligations. We will explain any necessary limitation; deleting our copy does not erase independent copies held by you or other providers.

07. Data security

Google states that Play Games Services encrypts the data described in its SDK disclosure in transit using HTTPS. This is transport encryption, not a claim of end-to-end encryption or that Google cannot read service data. The game does not add a separate encryption layer to local preferences and save files. Device protections depend on your operating system and settings.

No system guarantees complete security. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

08. Children's privacy

The game does not ask for a player's age or date of birth and has no integrated advertising. Google Play Games features are subject to Google's account eligibility, parental controls and service availability. Local gameplay remains available when sign-in is unavailable. This policy does not claim that Google services collect no children's data, assign the game's target audience, or certify Families compliance.

Parents and guardians with concerns can contact bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com. For Google-managed information, use Google's account and family controls as applicable.

09. Privacy enquiries & your rights

Contact bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com about this policy or information held by the studio. Depending on your location and circumstances, rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection may apply. Describe the game and your request without sending unnecessary identification documents.

Use Android controls for local storage and backups, and Google's controls for Google-managed profile and game data. We will explain which information the studio can act on and which requests need to be directed to Google.

10. Effective date & policy updates

This revision is dated 3 November 2026 and covers the Google Play Games-enabled update. Earlier offline releases did not include these online features. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Any future advertising, purchase or other material data-processing change will be reflected in the policy and Google Play disclosures before release, with any additional notice or consent where required.
