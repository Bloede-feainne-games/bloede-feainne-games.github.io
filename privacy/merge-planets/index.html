01. Developer & contact information

Bloede Feainne Games develops Merge Planets: Make a Galaxy!, an Android physics puzzle game made with Unity. This policy applies to version 0.1 of the game, package com.feainne.mergeplanets, and explains related website and support communications separately.

For privacy enquiries or game support, email bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com.

02. What information is processed

This version of the game does not collect or share user data off the device. It has no account registration, sign-in, cloud-save service, advertising, or in-app purchases. Its Android configuration removes the app’s Internet permission, and Unity Analytics and Diagnostics are disabled.

The game processes scores, preferences, and the device’s interface language locally to run gameplay and present the interface. It does not upload scores, preferences, device or advertising identifiers, location, gameplay events, crash reports, or diagnostic information to the studio or an embedded service.

Opening this policy in your browser, Android’s own backup features, and emailing the studio involve separate services. They are described below so the game’s offline behavior is not confused with those activities.

03. Local gameplay storage

The game uses Unity’s PlayerPrefs preference storage on the device to remember:

  • Your best score.
  • Whether sound is enabled.
  • Whether vibration is enabled.
  • Your chosen game language.

The current score is processed in memory during play. When there is no saved language choice, the game reads the current interface-language setting to select a supported language. Game and localization assets load from local content. The studio does not receive or remotely synchronize these values.

Android backup: Android may back up or transfer app preferences depending on your device, account, and backup settings. The game does not operate a cloud-save server or control those copies. See Android’s backup documentation for platform behavior.

04. Unity & third-party services

The game uses the Unity engine and local packages for rendering, input, localization, and asset loading. Unity Engine Diagnostics, Unity Analytics, Cloud Diagnostics reporting, and Performance Reporting are disabled for this version. No Unity Cloud project is linked. These integrations do not send player telemetry or diagnostic reports from this version.

Firebase, AdMob, authentication, cloud-save, and billing SDKs are not integrated. The optional translation-feedback destination is unconfigured, so the game does not open a feedback form or send prefilled language information.

Unity’s general provider information is available in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy; its inclusion here does not mean that the disabled services are active.

Privacy-policy website: choosing Privacy Policy opens this public page in your external browser. The game adds no player identifiers, score, language, or other player information to the URL. The browser makes its own request to GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as explained in GitHub Pages documentation. The studio’s website includes no analytics or tracking scripts.

Support email: if you choose to email the studio, we receive your address, any sender name, message, attachments, and normal email metadata. The public address is hosted by Microsoft Outlook. Microsoft processes correspondence under its Privacy Statement. This email exchange happens outside the game.

Future monetization: AdMob and a one-time Remove Ads purchase are planned for a later update. Neither is implemented in this version. The policy and Google Play disclosures will be updated for the actual integrations before they are released.

05. Purposes of processing & sharing

Local processing runs gameplay, displays scores, remembers your best score, and applies language, sound, and vibration preferences. It does not create a studio-held player profile.

Information you voluntarily send by email is used to handle your enquiry, provide relevant support, follow up on that issue, and handle privacy requests. The email provider processes the correspondence to deliver and operate email. It is not an in-game analytics or advertising integration.

No gameplay data is uploaded to or shared with advertising, analytics, account, or cloud-save providers by this game version. Browser hosting and operating-system backups remain subject to the relevant providers’ policies and your settings.

06. Data retention & deletion

The best score and preferences have no automatic expiry in the game. They remain in app storage until replaced or removed. You can reset them using Android’s option to clear this app’s storage. Option names vary by device, and this action resets your local score and settings.

Uninstalling normally removes the app’s local storage. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not delete email you sent or guarantee deletion of Android backup copies. Manage platform backups through the relevant device or account controls; a backup may restore preferences when the app is installed again.

The studio retains support and privacy correspondence for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com. Any necessary retention limitation will be explained when handling the request; deletion from the studio’s correspondence does not erase copies held by you or independently controlled by providers.

There is no game account or server-side save to delete, and the studio cannot remotely retrieve or clear your on-device preferences.

07. Data security

The game stores simple scores and settings in the device’s app preference storage. It does not add a separate encryption layer to these values. Device and operating-system protections also depend on your device settings.

Because this version does not upload user data from the app, it makes no claim about encrypting an app telemetry or cloud-save connection. Browser requests and email use separate services and their own security arrangements. No system can guarantee complete security; please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

08. Children’s privacy

This version does not ask players to provide personal information or create an account. It has no advertising or off-device gameplay data collection, and does not ask for a player’s age or date of birth.

If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, or believe personal information was provided to the studio in a message, contact bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com so we can review and address it. This policy does not assign an age rating or claim a children’s privacy certification.

09. Privacy enquiries & your rights

Contact bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com to ask about this policy or request access, correction, or deletion of information held in studio correspondence. Depending on where you live and the circumstances, other privacy rights may apply. Requests will be considered under applicable requirements, including any information needed to identify the relevant correspondence.

Please identify the game and describe your request without sending unnecessary identification documents or sensitive information. Manage on-device preferences and Android backups through your device or account controls; there is no studio player account or remotely stored gameplay record.

10. Effective date & policy updates

This policy takes effect on 31 October 2026. Changes will be published at this URL with an updated date. If a future release changes data processing, including adding advertising or purchases, the policy and Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated before that release, with any additional notice or consent provided where required.
