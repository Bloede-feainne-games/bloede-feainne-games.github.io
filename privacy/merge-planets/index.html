01. Developer & contact information

Bloede Feainne Games develops Merge Planets: Make a Galaxy!, an Android physics puzzle game, package com.feainne.mergeplanets. This policy explains local gameplay storage, Google Play Games features, optional rewarded advertising, age screening and privacy choices. It applies to test and production releases using the processing described here. Advertising availability depends on the installed release, age category, region, network availability and applicable privacy choices.

Alpha and future releases: the Alpha Android build reviewed on 5 November 2026 uses Google test ads and does not contain Yandex Mobile Ads or AppMetrica. The conditional Yandex provisions below describe a planned adult-only integration; they do not mean that those providers receive data from that Alpha build. A provider can process data through the game only if it is included and enabled in the installed release and the conditions described below are met.

For privacy enquiries or game support, email bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com.

02. What information is processed

Processing by the game: the game stores progress, preferences and age-screen records on your device. It exchanges player and gameplay information with Google Play Games for online features. The studio does not operate a separate player-account or gameplay-data server. Local play remains available if sign-in or a connection is unavailable.

  • Player identity: the Google Play Games Player ID, gamer display name and avatar. The game retrieves player profiles to display the leaderboard and keeps these display details in memory while it is viewed. The game does not ask for your Google password or request your email address, contacts or friends list.
  • Gameplay: completed-run scores for the leaderboard, unlocked achievements and cumulative achievement progress. Pending submissions can be stored locally and retried later.
  • Cloud saves: best score, earned achievements and their progress, Cosmic Atlas discoveries, the laser inventory and its reward/spending history, daily-gift dates, and a game snapshot containing score, planet levels, positions and movement, current and next planet, gameplay timers, laser heat, and save-revision and integrity metadata. These are linked to the signed-in Play Games player. Sound, vibration and language preferences are not included in our cloud-save payload.
  • Age and privacy settings: local age categories, associated dates and service records are explained in section 03. Advertising services receive age-treatment and privacy signals. UMP manages consent status and choices in SDK storage; these can include purpose and provider choices and consent strings. The game checks the resulting advertising permissions rather than treating an age answer as advertising consent.

Processing by Google: Play Games processes the service information above and its own SDK analytics and diagnostics for reliability and product improvement. See Google's Play Games data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

When advertising services are activated, Google Mobile Ads processes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, performance diagnostics, and device or account identifiers where applicable. Its SDK disclosure includes app set identifiers and advertising identifiers when available. The game removes the Android advertising-ID permission and disables publisher first-party identifiers and publisher personalization; other technical identifiers and network data can still be processed. This processing supports advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, including for Google test ads. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Conditional processing by Yandex: if the adult-only integration described in section 04 is enabled, Yandex receives network connection information, including the IP address used to contact its service, and advertising-request information. Its Mobile Ads data disclosure also identifies device identifiers where the relevant access is permitted, for advertising delivery and measurement. Its default SDK disclosure does not list collection of app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics or device location. Any additional AppMetrica analytics, location collection or other processing beyond what is described here requires updated disclosures before it is enabled.

The game does not request GPS location, contacts, microphone, camera or phone-state access. It does not send your Play Games Player ID, scores, cloud-save payload, entered age or age-record dates as custom advertising targeting or server-side reward-verification data. Age restrictions and non-personalized ads do not mean that providers process no data.

03. Local gameplay storage

Unity's PlayerPrefs stores your best score, sound and vibration choices, and selected game language. Without a saved language choice, the game reads the device's interface language to choose a supported language.

Local files preserve the unfinished board and gameplay timers, with recovery copies for interrupted writes. Separate synchronization records keep the linked Player ID, queued scores, achievement progress, upload acknowledgements and save revisions. The canonical laser wallet also records gifts, rewarded-ad receipts, spending and tutorial state. Confirmed ad callbacks are journalled locally so an interrupted write can be recovered without granting the same reward twice. These local receipt IDs are not Google account credentials or a server-verified advertising receipt. The display names and avatars fetched for rankings are not written into our save-file format. Google SDKs may keep their own service caches and privacy settings in app storage. An earlier device snapshot may be retained when a cloud game replaces it.

Local play and saving continue when online services fail. An unexpected shutdown may recover an earlier checkpoint. A restart or game over clears the active run, but does not erase best score, achievements, laser inventory, gift history, synchronization records, recovery copies or Google-held data.

Age screening and local age records

The neutral screen asks for your age in completed years when you first choose an advertising action. It is not shown during ordinary game startup. The input starts empty, no date of birth is requested, and the entered number is cleared from the form. You may select Skip: this cancels that advertising action, saves an unknown-age status and leaves local play and the free daily gift available. A later voluntary advertising action can use the restricted Google-only route for unknown age.

The game saves a child (under 13), teen (13–17), adult (18+) or unknown category; whether an answer or refusal was recorded; estimated dates for age-category and advertising-treatment eligibility transitions; the decision date; a correction date set 30 days later; and a record version and integrity tag. The entered number and date of birth are not stored directly. However, the derived dates can reveal an estimate of the age entered for a minor. These categories are technical safeguards, not universal legal definitions or proof of identity or parental authorization.

Annual age review: a saved child or teen decision becomes unknown after one calendar year unless the player updates it. The game does not infer adulthood from estimated dates or automatically show a new questionnaire when the record expires. Until age is entered again through the age settings, the stricter unknown-age advertising route applies. Adult decisions do not expire annually. Missing, invalid or unreadable records also use unknown-age restrictions. This age-review schedule is separate from advertising consent checks, which can be needed in every new app session.

Use Age and player in the pause menu to update the record or prepare the device for another player. Opening it resets the current record to unknown and temporarily stops new ad requests while the correction is open. Selecting Skip keeps unknown-age restrictions; accepting an age updates the category if the correction cooldown permits it. Corrections are subject to the 30-day limit. This is not deletion: the cooldown and older local backup or recovery records can remain.

Age records are not included in Play Games cloud saves or rankings. Advertising SDKs receive the applicable age-treatment and privacy signals instead of the entered age and stored dates. To remove the local age records and recovery copies, clear the app's storage in Android settings; this also removes local gameplay data. The game has no dedicated age-only deletion button or remote age-record editor.

Android backup: Android may back up or transfer app data depending on device and account settings. This can include age metadata and recovery files; the game does not expressly exclude them from operating-system backup. These copies are separate from Play Games Saved Games. The studio does not control Android backup retention. See Android's backup documentation.

04. Advertising, Google, Yandex & other services

Google Play Games: authentication is attempted automatically. After successful sign-in, scores, achievements and cloud saves can synchronize automatically; using a Sync button is not the only way transmission starts. There is no in-game switch that disables every Google SDK operation. Google may offer to create a Play Games profile, but the studio does not create a separate username/password account. Google account and Play Games controls are managed through Google.

Leaderboards and achievements may show your gamer identity and game results to others according to Google's features and your profile visibility settings. Cloud snapshots are associated with your signed-in game data and are not public leaderboard records.

Optional rewarded advertising

You can choose to watch a rewarded ad for additional lasers, including an optional daily-gift bonus or last-chance laser, or to restore an eligible missed day in the reward calendar. A free daily-gift option remains available. There is no compulsory advertising after a loss. The game does not show banners or automatic game-over interstitials. Rewards depend on the advertising SDK confirming that the reward was earned; closing an ad alone does not grant one.

Google AdMob: rewarded advertising uses two age routes. Children, teenagers and players of unknown age use a Google-only route with age restrictions. Adults aged 18 or older use an adult route. Google requests use non-personalized advertising and a maximum ad-content rating of G. Child and unknown-age profiles receive child-directed treatment and an under-age-of-consent signal. Teenagers receive child or teen treatment according to the configured age threshold, with the stricter treatment when eligibility is not established. Adult status does not override consent requirements.

Advertising is activated by a voluntary advertising action. Age and privacy settings are applied before initialization and ad requests. A privacy-status check or ad preload can contact providers before an ad is displayed, and another ad may preload after one closes. Test ads also use the real Google SDK and network services. Availability of a reward button does not guarantee an ad will be available.

Yandex Mobile Ads — conditional adult mediation: a release that includes and enables this planned integration may use it through AdMob Mediation for adults aged 18 or older in Russia, subject to network availability. It is excluded from the child, teen and unknown-age routes. It must not be initialized or allowed to process data through the game until the age, region, applicable provider-specific consent and safe-initialization conditions are satisfied. If those conditions cannot be established or maintained, including after a player change or withdrawal of consent, Yandex must remain unavailable. AdMob's ability to request an ad is not by itself permission to use Yandex. The Alpha build identified in section 01 contains neither Yandex Mobile Ads nor AppMetrica.

AppMetrica: Yandex's SDK can include AppMetrica components. Their presence and any processing in a future enabled integration must be assessed together with Yandex; the studio does not currently run a separate AppMetrica game-analytics service. Including such components would not mean that they are allowed to collect data from children, teenagers or unknown-age players. Additional analytics or data uses beyond this policy require updated disclosures before activation.

Provider policies: Google Privacy Policy and Yandex Privacy Policy. See section 09 for consent and privacy choices.

Laser shop: the implemented shop displays laser-pack options. In the Alpha configuration covered by this revision it is a preview: real-money purchases and purchase initialization are disabled, it displays no live prices, and it cannot charge you or grant a paid purchase. This policy does not represent a planned payment flow as active. Enabling payments requires a separate review and disclosure of the actual purchase-data processing.

Google Play updates: the in-app update integration checks availability through Google Play and can offer a Play-managed update. It does not send your saved board or laser wallet as an update request.

Unity: the engine provides rendering, input, localization and asset loading. Unity Analytics, Engine Diagnostics, Cloud Diagnostics reporting and Performance Reporting are disabled. This does not disable Google services or their own diagnostics. See the Unity player privacy policy for provider information.

Website: the game opens this policy in an external browser using a fixed URL without player information appended. GitHub Pages receives normal web requests, including IP addresses for security; see GitHub's hosting information. Our website has no analytics or tracking scripts.

Support email: if you contact the studio, we receive your address, sender name if supplied, message, attachments and normal email metadata. Microsoft Outlook processes correspondence under its Privacy Statement. This exchange takes place outside the game.

05. Purposes of processing & sharing

The game uses local information to run gameplay, remember preferences, restore progress, apply age restrictions and prevent duplicate rewards. Google Play Games receives identity and gameplay information to authenticate players, operate rankings and achievements, and synchronize or restore saves. Its SDK analytics and diagnostics support reliability and product improvement.

Google's advertising processing supports ad delivery and measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. If the conditional Yandex route is enabled, its advertising processing supports delivery and measurement under the conditions in section 04. Consent information is used to determine which advertising operations are permitted and to apply your choices. The game does not use its saved board, achievement history or laser wallet to build an advertising profile.

Data sent through integrated providers is processed outside your device even though the studio has no separate gameplay-data backend. Providers may process information in countries other than yours, under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Support correspondence is used to respond to enquiries, provide follow-up and handle privacy requests.

06. Data retention & deletion

On your device: active checkpoints are replaced as play progresses. Best score, preferences, achievements, laser and gift history, queued submissions, synchronization metadata, age-screen records and recovery copies have no fixed automatic deletion period. Expiry of a minor’s age decision changes its advertising treatment; it does not erase the underlying record. Pending confirmed-ad journal entries are removed after their reward is safely reflected in the wallet; the receipt remains in the reward history for deduplication. Clear this app's storage in Android settings to remove these local records together. Uninstalling normally removes app storage, but does not remove Google-held data, support email or all possible backup copies.

Google-held game data: use the Google Play Games profile controls to delete individual game data or your Play Games profile. Google's deletion instructions explain the available web and Play Store routes. For individual game data, choose Merge Planets: Make a Galaxy! in the game-data deletion list. Google controls its processing times, retention exceptions and any separately managed Play History; we do not promise a fixed deletion period or the deletion of all Google service records.

Advertising and privacy data: Google and, when used by mediation, Yandex control retention of their SDK and service records under their policies. The studio cannot promise a fixed retention period or remotely erase every provider record. Use Google's data and privacy controls, the advertising privacy options when offered, or the Yandex privacy contact and rights procedures for Yandex-held information. Changing a privacy choice is not the same as deleting previously collected data. Clearing app storage removes local SDK preferences but does not itself delete server records.

Preventing old progress from returning: stop the game on all devices and review Google's game sign-in/access settings before deleting online data. Clear the game's local storage on those devices and review Android backups as well. Deleting only the cloud copy leaves local saves and queued uploads; later sign-in or synchronization can recreate game data from those copies. Restarting a run, going offline, or removing access alone does not delete previously transmitted information.

The current game has no dedicated delete-cloud-data button and the studio does not operate a separate player-account database. Emailing the studio does not let us erase your Google account, every advertising-provider record or remotely clear your device. We can help identify the relevant controls.

Studio correspondence: email bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com to request access, correction or deletion of messages held by the studio. Correspondence is retained as needed for the enquiry, related follow-up or applicable legal obligations. We will explain any necessary limitation; deleting our copy does not erase independent copies held by you or other providers.

07. Data security

Google states that Play Games Services uses HTTPS and Mobile Ads uses TLS for the data covered by their disclosures; Yandex describes HTTPS/TLS for the data in its Mobile Ads disclosure. This is transport encryption, not a claim of end-to-end encryption or that the service providers cannot read service data. Local integrity and duplicate-reward checks are not encryption or a guarantee against tampering. The game does not add a separate confidentiality-encryption layer to local preferences and save files. Device protections depend on your operating system and settings.

No system guarantees complete security. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

08. Children's privacy

Merge Planets is intended for an audience aged 6 and older, including minors. Children, teenagers and players of unknown age use the restricted Google-only advertising route. Yandex is not permitted for any of these categories. Unknown age is not treated as an adult or as consent. Skipping the age question does not prevent local play or collection of the free daily gift.

The game applies age-treatment signals before ad initialization and requests, disables advertising personalization and publisher first-party identifiers, and removes the Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions. It invalidates earlier advertising operations and discards cached ads when the age route or privacy permissions change. These restrictions do not mean that Google processes no technical data or that already transmitted information is erased.

Advertising made available to children and unknown-age users must meet the applicable Google Play Families requirements, including eligible advertising SDKs, restricted data use and appropriate ad content and presentation. These are requirements for serving ads, not a claim that this policy independently certifies the app, an SDK configuration or every advertisement. An age answer is not parental consent.

Advertising age screening does not disable Google Play Games authentication or synchronization. Those services are subject to Google's account eligibility, family controls and availability. Local play remains available without successful sign-in. Parents and guardians can use Google's account and family controls for Google-managed information and contact bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com about the game or this policy.

09. Advertising privacy choices, enquiries & your rights

Advertising privacy choices

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to refresh advertising privacy status and display a configured privacy message when required. In the on-demand advertising flow, this happens when advertising is first activated in each new app session, before ad initialization and loading, and when the advertising profile needs a new check. UMP may require a renewed choice based on region or changed or expired consent. Keeping an adult age decision does not bypass these checks; the annual review of minors' age is unrelated to consent validity.

When UMP requires a privacy-options entry, the pause menu offers Ad privacy choices. Use it to change or withdraw the choices supported by the applicable message. The game discards cached ads when the form opens and allows further requests only if the updated status permits them. An options-only path is retained when an age change temporarily stops advertising, without initializing or loading ads through that path. In a new session, the on-demand UMP check determines whether the entry is required.

Messages and available choices depend on your region and the applicable privacy requirements. Selecting an age, requesting a reward, or accepting this policy is not consent to every advertising purpose. Where consent is required, the relevant provider and purposes must be covered before that processing starts. Declining personalization can still permit non-personalized or limited advertising and associated technical processing. The game does not interpret UMP's permission to request Google ads as consent for a future Yandex integration.

For Yandex to become available, any required provider-specific choices must be included in the applicable consent process and respected before initialization and subsequent requests. That future integration must also respect withdrawal and player changes; its configuration in an AdMob mediation group alone does not satisfy these conditions. Until implemented and verified, the Yandex route remains disabled.

You can continue local play without choosing rewarded advertising. If a required privacy form is unavailable, contact the studio or use the provider privacy controls linked above. Changing a choice does not delete earlier provider records, and clearing app storage is not a substitute for withdrawing consent or requesting deletion from a provider. SDK consent settings can remain locally until changed or app storage is cleared.

Depending on your location and the processing involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your data, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and complain to a relevant data-protection authority. Withdrawal affects future consent-based processing and does not itself undo processing that occurred before withdrawal. These rights can have legal limits.

For requests concerning information held by the studio, email bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com. Describe the game and your request without sending unnecessary identification documents. For device storage, Google-managed information or advertising-provider records, use the controls in section 06. We can explain which information we can act on and which requests need to go to the relevant provider.

10. Effective date & policy updates

This revision is dated 5 November 2026. It describes the processing above without depending on particular SDK versions, achievement counts or interface layouts. Moving from test ads to live ads, or enabling the conditional Yandex route, does not by itself change these disclosures if the actual providers, data, purposes, audience restrictions and privacy controls remain within the conditions stated here.

If those practices change materially, we will update this policy at the same URL and the relevant Google Play disclosures before introducing the changed processing, with additional notice or consent where required. A provider's inclusion in this policy does not authorize processing that the installed game cannot safely support.
