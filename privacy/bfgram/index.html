BFGram privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Developer: Bloede Feainne · Android package: com.bloedefeainne.bfgram

BFGram is an independent, unofficial messaging client that uses the Telegram API and is part of the Telegram ecosystem. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram. There is no separate BFGram messaging account: you sign in to your Telegram account.

This policy explains the BFGram Android client and information you send directly to Bloede Feainne. Telegram operates the underlying messaging service and describes its handling of account and service data in the Telegram privacy policy and Telegram terms.

Information used to provide the app

  • Account and authentication: your phone number, Telegram account identifier, profile information, and login information are processed to sign in and use Telegram. Authentication codes and any two-step verification password go through Telegram’s authentication flow. Never send them to studio support.
  • Messages and content: messages, channel and group membership, photos, videos, recordings, files, and other content you choose to send or access are processed by the app and Telegram to provide messaging and synchronization.
  • Contacts: if you grant permission and enable contact synchronization, contact names and phone numbers are used to find contacts on Telegram and synchronize with Telegram. You can manage synchronization and delete synchronized contacts in the app’s privacy settings.
  • Optional device features: camera, microphone, media and file access support capturing and sending content and making calls. Location permission supports maps and location sharing that you choose to use. Live location can operate in the background while you keep it active. Android settings allow you to revoke permissions; affected features may then stop working.
  • Technical information: connection information, IP address, device and app details, session information, notification tokens, and service diagnostics may be processed by Telegram or the service providers involved to operate, authenticate, secure, and deliver the service.

BFGram does not introduce a studio-operated messaging server or an additional studio analytics or advertising SDK. Local settings, session information, databases, and cached media are stored on your device. The Subscription Manager and own-message deletion tools send user-requested operations to Telegram; they do not create a studio database of your chats or subscriptions.

Service providers, other users, and third-party content

Telegram receives the information necessary to operate your Telegram account and the messaging features you use. Recipients, group members, channel administrators, bots, and mini apps may receive information when you interact with them, according to your actions and Telegram’s settings. Public content can be visible to people beyond your contacts.

The Android client uses Google services, including Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications and Google Maps for supported map features. These services can process installation or notification identifiers, device and connection information, and data needed for the feature. See Firebase privacy and security information and Google’s privacy policy. Google Analytics is not enabled for the BFGram Firebase project.

Web pages, bots, mini apps, payment providers, and other external services you open have their own policies. This policy does not give Bloede Feainne control over those services. Data may be processed outside your country as described by the providers.

Advertising and payments

Bloede Feainne does not add its own advertising to BFGram. Telegram’s official sponsored messages can appear in supported channels, according to Telegram’s rules and account eligibility. Other users may also post promotional content. The initial BFGram test build does not offer studio-operated purchases or subscriptions.

Storage, security, and retention

The client uses Telegram’s encrypted transport for Telegram communications. Cloud chats are hosted by Telegram; they are not all end-to-end encrypted. Supported secret chats use Telegram’s separate end-to-end encryption mechanism. Device access and the services you choose to use also affect privacy and security.

Local app data remains until you clear it, log out, uninstall the app, or an app retention or cache setting removes it. Downloaded files saved outside app storage may remain on your device. Telegram controls service-side retention under its policy and account settings; deleting local app data does not delete your Telegram account. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations; you can request deletion of correspondence held by the studio.

Delete your account or data

Deleting a Telegram account affects your Telegram account across all clients. If you only want to stop using BFGram, log out and uninstall BFGram instead.
  1. Remove local BFGram data: log out, then use Android Settings → Apps → BFGram → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall BFGram. Delete separately saved downloads if you also want to remove those files.
  2. Delete your Telegram account and associated Telegram data: open Telegram’s account deletion page, sign in with the account you want to delete, and follow Telegram’s confirmation steps. BFGram links to this resource from Settings → Delete account and data. The app’s privacy settings also contain Telegram’s automatic account deletion setting.
  3. Delete information held by studio support: email bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com with the subject “BFGram data deletion request”. Identify the correspondence or data concerned. We may need enough information to verify the request. Do not send passwords, login codes, or identity documents unless a justified secure process has been agreed.

Bloede Feainne cannot sign in to or delete your Telegram account for you. Telegram describes any retained data and copies held by other users in its data deletion policy. Removing an account does not automatically cancel a subscription you bought from Telegram or another provider.

Age and acceptable use

Follow Telegram’s minimum-age requirements for your location and the applicable app-store rating. BFGram is not designed for children. Do not use it for spam, scams, abuse, illegal content, or activities forbidden by Telegram. Telegram’s existing reporting and blocking tools remain available for supported content and interactions.

Contact, privacy requests, and changes

Developer: Bloede Feainne. For BFGram support, privacy questions, access or correction requests, or deletion requests for data held by the studio, contact bloedefeainne.games@outlook.com. For Telegram-controlled account or service data, use Telegram’s privacy tools and the contact routes in its policy.

We will update this page when the app’s practices change and show the effective date here.
